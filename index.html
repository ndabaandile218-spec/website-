ABOUT US

A Comfortable Stay in Musgrave

🏆 Booking.com Award of Excellence, every year since 2014

Musgrave Avenue Guest Lodge occupies a beautifully renovated Georgian-style building in Musgrave, Berea — a short walk from Musgrave Shopping Centre and a five-minute drive from the Durban CBD and the beachfront. Our rooms and self-catering suites are tastefully decorated, several with their own kitchenettes, and an English breakfast is available on request. It's a quiet, well-located base for business trips, family stays, and everything in between.
